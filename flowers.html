About the Rose

The Rose is one of the most popular and beautiful flowers in the world. It is widely known for its beauty, fragrance and variety of colors.

Common Name: Rose
Scientific Name: Rosa
Family: Rosaceae
Colors: Red, Pink, White, Yellow and Orange
Fragrance: Sweet and pleasant
Habitat: Gardens, parks and temperate regions

Scientific Classification

Classification Details
Kingdom Plantae
Family Rosaceae
Genus Rosa

About the Sunflower

The Sunflower is a bright and beautiful flower known for its large flower head and its ability to grow toward sunlight. It is commonly found in gardens and agricultural fields.

Common Name: Sunflower
Scientific Name: Helianthus annuus
Family: Asteraceae
Colors: Yellow, Orange and Red
Fragrance: Mild
Habitat: Gardens, fields and sunny areas

Scientific Classification

Classification Details
Kingdom Plantae
Family Asteraceae
Genus Helianthus
Species Helianthus annuus

Lavender is a beautiful flowering plant known for its purple flowers and distinctive fragrance. It is widely appreciated for its pleasant aroma and is commonly grown in gardens.

Common Name: Lavender
Scientific Name: Lavandula
Family: Lamiaceae

Fragrance: Strong and pleasant
Habitat: Gardens, hills and dry regions

Scientific Classification

Classification Details
Kingdom Plantae
Family Lamiaceae
Genus Lavandula
Species Lavandula angustifolia

The Tulip is a colorful and attractive flower known for its simple shape and wide variety of colors. It is especially popular during the spring season.

Common Name: Tulip
Scientific Name: Tulipa
Family: Liliaceae
Colors: Red, Pink, Yellow, White and Purple
Fragrance: Mild or pleasant
Habitat: Gardens, fields and temperate regions

Scientific Classification

Classification Details
Kingdom Plantae
Family Liliaceae
Genus Tulipa
Species Tulipa gesneriana
